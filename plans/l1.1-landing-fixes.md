# L1.1 — Landing page corrections (Oct 21, 2026)

**Jordan + Claude, Oct 21, 2026, from an audit of L1 as shipped (v281, PR #11).** The landing page works on the live site:
- routing, the film (desktop and 4:5, byte ranges, plays once) and the service-worker bypass;
- every image and every link;
- no console errors and no horizontal scroll at 390 or 1440 px.

Five things on the public page should change before it's shared widely, plus one docs fix. Frontend only: `index.html` and two docs files. No database, no functions.

## What ships

1. **Pricing without placeholders.** The public page shows "$—" and "Price not set" on Plus, Team and Facility, and "Choose Plus / Choose Team" open the free sign-up as if those plans existed. Until Joel sets prices:
   - show the **Free** card only, with one line beside it: "Paid plans for families, teams and facilities are coming.";
   - keep the Plus / Team / Facility cards in the file, `hidden`, ready for L1 Stage D;
   - the headline "Charting is free." and its subline stay.
2. **"Talk to us" goes nowhere.** It jumps to the FAQ, which has no contact. Replace it with a line under the Free card: "Running a facility? Email nate@knuckleballonline.com" (the address `privacy.html` already publishes), with the address as selectable text and a `mailto:` link.
3. **Arm care teaser hidden.** "Coming next · Arm care, next to the charts" is a product promise on the public page. Hide the section (`hidden`) until Joel wants to announce it. Nothing in the nav links to it.
4. **FAQ "Who sees a pitcher's charts?" is incomplete.** Today it says the pitcher, their coaches and the parents, and links the policy. `privacy.html` (Version 2026-10-17) says Knuckleball may also license or sell charting data and statistics. Proposed answer (legal wording, so Joel approves or edits it before it's built):

   > In the app: the pitcher, the coaches on their team and the parents on the account. Knuckleball doesn't sell or license charting data today, but our privacy policy keeps that option open: charting data and statistics, with the player's name, may be licensed to teams, programs, organizations, scouts and analytics companies. Email addresses, passwords, photos and coach notes are never sold, and a player added by a parent is only included if the parent agrees.

   Every claim was checked against `privacy.html` "How we may use and license charting data" (Version 2026-10-17):
   - "We don't do this today, and we may never do it";
   - the recipients: teams, programs, organizations, scouts and analytics companies;
   - data attributed with name, team, sport, level, jersey number and throwing hand;
   - never sold or licensed: email addresses, passwords, age brackets, consent records, profile photos, coach notes;
   - parent-added players only if the parent separately agrees.

   Re-check at build time.
5. **Old picker links at the root.** `index.html`'s router forwards a stored session and auth parameters to `signin.html`, but not `?sport=`, `?role=` or `?choose=`. A device still on the v280 shell (its `login.html` bounces to `index.html?choose=1`), or an old link or bookmark with `?sport=`, now lands on the landing page instead of the picker. Add `sport|role|choose` to the router's query test so these go to `signin.html` with the query intact. Section anchors (`#pricing`, `#faq` …) stay exempt.
6. **Docs: the outfield logo.**
   - `docs/cf-logo-mown.md` and `docs/cf-logo-mown-demo.html` name the home plate in their text, but their SVG draws the keypad (`kb-spot`).
   - The decision (Jordan + Joel, Oct 21): **The spot** (keypad) is the main logo; **Strikeout** (home plate) is the secondary, for uses like the outfield grass.
   - Replace the symbols with `kb-plate` / `kb-plate-lit`: the SVG and steps posted in #kb-build on Oct 21.
   - Re-render the demo PNG.

## Checked and fine (no change)

- `signin.html` is today's picker, unchanged apart from `noindex` and the `#signup` hook. `login.html`, `join.html` and `join-coach.html` point to it.
- Email links (reports, account deletion and export) go to `login.html` and `report.html`, never the root.
- `sw.js`: `/signin.html` is precached, `/media/hero-*` and `/media/shots/*` are never cached, and the cache is at v281.
- The approved copy (hero, gold band, How it works line) is exact. No requests go to another host.
