The two palettes

Baseball: navy, Carolina blue, white. Navy carries most of the weight; Carolina blue is the accent: the underline on a selected choice, the Scoreboard bar, and Next pitch in styles 2 and 3.

Softball: Carolina blue, pink, white. Pink is the main action (Next pitch); Carolina blue marks the selected choice. White or pink text on Carolina blue is too faint outdoors, so a dark navy-slate does the job of text color (and the dark backgrounds in styles 3 and 4).

Suggested starting point

Style 4 on the live charting screens, style 2 everywhere else. They share square corners, so the app still reads as one design. The scoreboard look reads fast in a dugout; the squared look keeps forms and settings calm.

A suggestion from the design pass, not a decision. Joel picks.

What changes if we adopt this

  • Baseball moves off green and gold; the softball theme in the app today (navy bar, pink, blue) is replaced.
  • The logo, reports and landing page should switch at the same time, so the app never shows two color schemes.
  • The condensed type in style 4 would be a self-hosted font, like the app's other fonts.

References: Nike · Under Armour · Strava · WHOOP. Style 4 is an original take on a TV score graphic; style 5 follows the iPhone's built-in controls.
