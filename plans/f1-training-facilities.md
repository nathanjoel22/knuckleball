## Goal

A training facility is a third kind of account beside Pitcher and Coach. A **Facility Manager** runs it, gets a baseball side and a softball side, invites pitching coaches onto each side by link, and sees every pitcher taking lessons there (70–100 at a time at the first facility). Each **pitching coach** sees only the pitchers assigned to them. Parents join by scanning a **QR code** printed at the front desk, which drops them into the existing parent-creates-the-player flow. Facility pitchers, their guardians and their coach talk in **Chat**, which replaces coach notes inside facilities; the manager can read everything and can post to the whole facility. Everyone, facility or not, gets the pitcher bar (Charts · History · Trends · Chat · Profile) and a header **Back**.

The first facility is a **free pilot**; pricing is decided later (P3-01). `organizations` is where facility billing will hang.

**Wording:** the app never says "his/him" for a coach or pitcher — neutral wording throughout (the softball side is mostly girls). This document uses "they".

## What exists that this builds on

- **Teams, staffs, invites:** `teams` (one sport each, `teams.level`), `team_coaches` with head/assistant roles and **one head per team** (R6), per-team pitcher and coach invite tokens generated by trigger (R0, Amendment 9), joining only by a real invite (Oct 2 fixes), `sessions.team_id` must be a team the pitcher is on (G3 check), departures log, `pitcher_workload` server call (R4 roster line).
- **Visibility:** R4 — coaches of every non-archived team a pitcher is currently on can read all their sessions; only the recording team notes, reports or deletes.
- **Logins and profiles:** one login, many profiles, one per sport/role (S4 Stage A) with the top-right switcher; parent-created players with no login, the creating parent recorded as **`profiles.managed_by`** (S4 Stage B); age attestation, 13–17 guardian approval, under-13 only via a parent (P1-10); "Join as:" picker on invite links. **Role boundaries stay exactly as today (Joel, Oct 29):** a pitcher sign-in cannot open a coach or facility account, a coach sign-in cannot open a pitcher or facility account, a facility manager sign-in cannot open a coach or pitcher account; within a login, the existing rules for adding profiles in the same or the other sport, adding players as a parent, and forming teams in the other sport are unchanged. F1 adds a role; it changes none of this.
- **Notes:** `session_notes`, coaches of the pitcher's current team write, pitcher reads, shown live on report pages (S3).
- **Design:** D1 tokens, D2 Broadcast look, U13 segmented trays, P2-05 standalone polish (in-app Back exists for installed mode), **the phone bottom bar shipped in v310 and v315** (so F1-D is only what's left), W1 shells in `knuckleball-app`.
- **Stats:** SC1-A (Oct 28) writes `session_stats` at save; **SC1-B (Oct 29, v317, migration `20261029000000_sc1b_stats_v2`)** — History, Trends, report history numbers and the roster pitch counts read the saved stats instead of downloading pitches, and each pitcher's History is cached on the phone for offline. A 100-pitcher roster is the case this was built for.
- **Email:** Resend free tier (100/day) and H1 sending limits — no bulk email from chat in v1.

## Model (one team per side, per-coach groups inside it)

A facility is an **organization** that owns **two teams**, one per sport. Everything per-team keeps working unchanged (invites, G3, R4, reports). New: the organization, the coach group, and the manager, who is simply **the head coach of both facility teams**.

```
organizations        id, name, manager_login uuid, created_at
teams                + org_id (nullable FK; set for both facility teams), + kind ('team' default | 'facility')
team_coaches         unchanged: the manager holds role='head' on both facility teams; pitching coaches are 'assistant'
coach_groups         id, team_id, coach_id, join_token unique, created_at   -- one per coach per facility team, created when a coach joins
coach_group_members  group_id, pitcher_id, added_by, added_at, flagged_new bool   -- a pitcher may be in several groups
```

- **Why head, not a new role (Jordan):** every existing head check (H1 delete, P1-09 rename/archive, R6 handoff, invite regeneration) works for the manager with no change, and nothing is silently lost. `is_org_manager(team_id)` is just "is head of a team whose `org_id` is set".
- **Handoff:** the manager hands the facility to another coach through the existing R6 head-handoff on **both** teams at once (one action, one function `handoff_facility(new_manager)`), which also rewrites `organizations.manager_login`. With one manager, nothing happens if they leave — this is the only way out.
- **Facility sign-in and signup (Joel, Oct 29).** `signin.html` gets a **third card, Training facility, in the same place and order as the Pitcher and Coach cards**, with its own sign-in form. Where the pitcher and coach cards offer "create an account", the facility card instead says **"Facility accounts are set up by Knuckleball. Contact us for your signup link"** with the inquiry form (facility name, your name, email, phone, sport(s), roughly how many pitchers) that emails **nate@knuckleballonline.com** through an Edge Function under H1's limits, escaped; no account is created by the form. After the deal, **Joel issues a one-time signup link**: `facility-signup.html?t=<token>` from a `facility_invites (token, facility_name, manager_email, baseball_level, softball_level, expires_at, used_at)` row Joel creates. Opening the link lets the manager set their name and password (18+ attestation, policy links) and runs `create_facility(token)` — SECURITY DEFINER, consumes the token once, creates the org, both teams (`kind='facility'`), both coach profiles on the manager's login (baseball and softball), both head rows, and relies on the existing trigger for invite tokens; idempotent on retry; a used or expired token shows a plain message. The manager thereafter signs in on the **Training facility card**, which accepts only logins that own an organization (a pitcher or coach login is refused there, and a facility login is refused on the other two cards — role boundaries as today), and lands on the baseball side; the existing switcher flips sides.
- A pitching coach joins a side through that side's **coach invite link** (existing). Joining a `kind='facility'` team creates their `coach_groups` row. A coach may be on both sides (two profiles, as today).
- A pitcher joins a side through a **coach's** join link/QR. Joining adds the `pitcher_teams` membership (existing) **and** the `coach_group_members` row for that coach, **flagged `new`** (Joel, Oct 29: no pending-approval queue; the roster shows a "New" tag with one-tap Remove for the coach and the manager, and the manager gets an unread dot on the roster). A pitcher who later takes lessons from a second coach is added to that coach's group by the manager or by scanning the second coach's code; one membership, two groups.
- **No leaderboard on facility teams:** Leaders tab hidden for `kind='facility'`; `get_team_leaderboard` refuses facility teams.

### Who sees and does what

| | Facility manager (head of both sides) | Facility pitching coach | Coach of another team the pitcher is on | Pitcher / parent-guardians |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roster | every pitcher on both sides, grouped by coach, "New" tags, `pitcher_workload` line | only pitchers in their group(s) | own team only (unchanged) | — |
| Sessions (pens and games, any team) | all, read + chart | all sessions of **their** pitchers, read + chart (R4, narrowed to the group) | all sessions of the pitcher, read — **includes facility lessons; R4 stands (Joel, Oct 29: "this data is ours, not the facility's")**; disclosed in the policy text | own, all teams |
| Add pitcher to a group / move / remove / clear "New" | yes | own group only | — | — |
| Chart a lesson | yes (filed to the facility team) | yes | — | yes (own pen; may file it to the facility) |
| Reports | facility sessions | their pitchers' facility sessions | own team's sessions (unchanged) | own |
| Notes | none in facilities (Chat instead) | none in facilities | unchanged on team sessions | read team notes |
| Chat | **read every thread incl. archived (90 days); post to the whole facility** | post to all their pitchers' families or to one family; archive own threads | **none** | post to their coach only |
| Delete a session | manager or pitcher (head rule) | no | unchanged | pitcher yes |
| Rename / archive team / regenerate links / handoff | yes | no | — | — |

**RLS.** New helper `is_group_coach(pitcher_id)` (SECURITY DEFINER, STABLE) alongside `is_team_coach`. On `kind='facility'` teams an assistant reads/charts/reports a pitcher's rows only if `is_group_coach`; the head (manager) reads all; `kind='team'` behavior is byte-for-byte unchanged. No policy touches `teams` and `pitcher_teams` directly (42P17). **RLS matrix before/after on staging and production, every role × every table, is a required deliverable on A and C1**, with policy counts.

**Sessions.** A lesson is a normal session with `team_id` = the facility team; G3 passes because the pitcher is on that team. Add `sessions.charted_by` if absent (the preconditions report says), set by `sync_session` from the caller.

## QR join (F1-B)

- One code **per coach**, printed once, valid until regenerated. Encodes `https://knuckleballonline.com/join.html?c=<coach_join_token>` (the token on `coach_groups`, so the group is known at join). Scanning is just opening the link; no app needed.
- `join.html?c=` flow: facility name + coach name + photo → **"Which sport?"** (preselected if the coach is on one side) → **always the parent flow** (S4 Stage B): parent creates the login, adds the child, age question; **under 13** → player has no login, the parents are the chat members; **13–17** → the child may get a login later via guardian approval; the join creates `pitcher_teams` + `coach_group_members` (flagged new). Secondary link **"I'm the player (13 or older)"**: 18+ → existing pitcher join; 13–17 → P1-10 guardian-approval path (chart now; reports and chat gated until a guardian approves, which also gives the parent a foothold).
- **Standing-invite risk (Jordan):** mitigated by the "New" flag + one-tap Remove, the manager's roster dot, regenerate-on-demand, and the existing Auth signup limit (30 per 5 min). Not a pending queue — the whole point is no wait at the first lesson.
- **Printable sheet** `qr-print.html?c=`: facility name, side, coach name, the code, the URL in text, "Scan to join Coach ___'s staff at ___", letter-size, print CSS only; manager's roster has **Print all codes**. Ask the facility: color or black-and-white; one sheet per coach or one poster.
- QR rendering: a small **MIT-licensed** encoder vendored under `vendor/` (~5 KB, no network, works offline).
- Regenerate invalidates the old code — acceptance check with the old URL.

## Chat (F1-C1 and F1-C2) — Joel's model, Oct 29

**Entry.** The Chat tab opens the viewer's thread list. A **compose button (paper-and-pen icon, top right)** chooses who to write to; the choices depend on role:

- **Facility manager:** *Everyone at the facility* (every pitcher with a login, every parent-guardian, every coach, both sides) — the only thread the manager posts in. The manager otherwise **reads** every thread (including archived ones for 90 days) and never posts inside a family's or a coach's thread.
- **Pitching coach:** *All my pitchers* (the coach plus every pitcher in their group(s) and those pitchers' approved parent-guardians — one thread per coach per side), or *one pitcher's family* (the private thread).
- **Pitcher / parent-guardian:** *My coach* only — opens the private thread with that coach (one per coach if the pitcher has two). They cannot start a thread with anyone else.

**Thread kinds.** `facility` (one per org), `roster` (one per coach group), `private` (one per pitcher per coach). Membership is a table, not a delete: when a pitcher moves coaches, the old coach leaves the private thread and the new coach joins it and **can read its history**; roster-thread membership follows the group. Guardians follow the player.

**Who may speak.** In a `roster` thread the coach posts; families read, and each message has **Reply privately**, which opens that family's private thread (Jordan's call 1, accepted: no reply-all; a coach may switch a roster thread to open posting if they want it). In a `private` thread the coach, the pitcher (if they have a login) and **only the two parent-guardian roles** post. In the `facility` thread only the manager posts.

**Guardian roles (Joel, Oct 29).** The guardian link in a parent's Profile asks the joiner: **Parent/guardian** or **Friend/extended family**. **At most two parent/guardian roles per player** (the creator in `profiles.managed_by` is one; the table `player_guardians (player_id, profile_id, role 'guardian' | 'family')` holds the others, and the function refuses a third `guardian`). Only parent-guardians are chat members; `family` members see the player's sessions and reports and get no chat. Only the creator can remove a guardian or delete the player. Every joiner attests 18+.

**Content.** Text, up to 2,000 chars, plus **YouTube links rendered as tappable links that open in the system browser — never embedded**; other URLs stay plain text. No photos or video. **Pins:** a coach or manager can pin a message in a thread they post in; pinned messages show as a shelf at the top (cap 5) and are visible to every current member regardless of when they joined. **Report:** every message has Report → flags it to the manager with an unread dot. Hostile text proven inert (S3's proof).

**Delete and archive (Joel, Oct 29, replaces the Oct 28 decisions 4 and 5).** Deleting a single message shows "This message was deleted" to everyone; the text is kept server-side (visible to the manager and to us). A coach "deleting" a thread **archives** it: hidden from the coach's list, read-only, **openly visible to the manager for 90 days**, then **automatically cleared** by the daily job (messages and thread purged). This is also the account-deletion answer: an account purge (A1) removes the person from every thread and their messages are shown as "Deleted account" until the thread's own 90-day archive clear; threads with a departed pitcher are archived when the pitcher leaves the last group. **Departed families keep read-only access to their private thread** until it clears (Jordan's call 2, accepted). The 90-day retention and the manager's read access go in the policy text (version bump, Joel approves).

**Notifications.** C1 ships unread dots (name dot, tab badge, thread badge) and nothing else. **No urgent email (Joel, Oct 29).** Push lands as F1-C2b with the W1 shells (`device_tokens`, APNs/FCM registration from the Capacitor app, one Edge Function on message insert notifying members except the author). The web/PWA build gets no push in v1. **No read receipts / "Seen by" in v1.**

**Manager inbox.** Default shows the facility thread, reported messages, and every thread on the current side; a filter *Just my own trainees* narrows the list but never the access.

**Chat replaces notes in facilities only:** the Note box and "Coaches notes" report section are hidden for `kind='facility'` sessions; team sessions keep S3 exactly as they are. Non-facility coaches never see a Chat tab.

**Tables.** `chat_threads (id, org_id, kind, team_id, group_id, pitcher_id, created_at, archived_at, clear_after)`; `chat_members (thread_id, profile_id, role, joined_at, left_at, read_only bool)`; `chat_messages (id, thread_id, author_profile_id, body, created_at, deleted_at, pinned_at, pinned_by)`; `chat_reads (thread_id, profile_id, last_read_at)`; `chat_reports (message_id, reported_by, created_at, resolved_at)`. Insert policies encode the speaking rules above; the manager's insert is allowed only on the `facility` thread.
