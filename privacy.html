Privacy Policy

Knuckleball LLC · Effective October 3, 2026 · Version 2026-10-03b

Knuckleball is a pitch-charting app for baseball and softball pitchers and their coaches. It is run by Knuckleball LLC (“we”). This page explains what we store, who can see it, where it lives, and how to have it exported or deleted. Questions: nate@knuckleballonline.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (handled by our sign-in provider; we never see it), and your sport and role (pitcher, coach, or parent).
  • Age bracket: whether you told us you're 18 or older, or 13 to 17, and when. We never ask for or store a date of birth. For 13–17 players: a parent or guardian's email address and whether they approved.
  • Players added by a parent: the player's name, sport, and throwing hand, plus the parent's consent and when it was given. We store no email address, login, or age for a player added this way.
  • Player details: jersey number, throwing hand, pitch types, whether you use a radar gun, an optional profile photo, and up to three email addresses that reports can be sent to.
  • Charting data: every pitch charted — pitch type, target and location, velocity, result, delivery, time to plate — plus, for live games, counts, outs, runners and plays; session dates; and who charted each session.
  • Coach notes written on a session.
  • Team details: team names, rosters, coaching staff, level of play, and invite links.

Who can see it

  • You see everything in your own account, including any players you've added.
  • Your coaches — the head and assistant coaches of any team you're on — see your sessions charted for that team, your player details, and their notes. If you leave a team, its coaches keep the sessions charted while you were on it, but don't see anything you chart afterward. For a player added by a parent, they also see that the player is on a parent account.
  • Teammates on a baseball team see the team leaderboard, which shows rankings (peak velocity, accuracy, strike percentage) and pitch counts — never anyone's sessions, pitch charts, or notes. Softball has no leaderboard.
  • Anyone with a report link. A session report lives at a long, random web address. Anyone who has that link can view the report, and the report page also shows that session's current coach notes. Reports go only to the addresses on your account, but a link can be forwarded — treat it like a shared document.

These limits are enforced by the database itself (see “Data handling commitments” below), not just by the app's screens.

Where it lives

  • Supabase stores the database, sign-in, and report pages, in its US East (N. Virginia) region. Supabase states: “All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS.” (supabase.com/security)
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Cloudflare provides our domain's DNS and email routing.
  • Resend sends our emails (account verification, reports, guardian approval requests, and roster notices).

We use no other service providers.

No tracking, advertising, or analytics

Knuckleball has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Our web pages load only files from knuckleballonline.com itself (including our fonts) and talk only to our Supabase database. To work without a connection, the app keeps your sign-in and any not-yet-synced sessions in your browser's own storage; we don't use tracking cookies, so there's nothing for a cookie banner to ask about.

Age and children

  • You must be 13 or older to create your own account.
  • If you're 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must approve your account by an emailed link. Until they do, you can chart, but you can't view or send reports.
  • A parent or legal guardian can create a parent account and add their child as a player, at any age, including under 13. The parent holds the account and its login, gives consent when adding the player, and receives the player's reports. The child gets no email address or login of their own.
  • Players under 13 can use Knuckleball only through a parent account.
  • Coaches and parents must be 18 or older.

If we learn that someone under 13 has created their own account, or that a player's age was given incorrectly, we'll contact the account's email and delete the account and its data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Saved sessions are final: pitch data can't be edited after a session is saved.
  • A pitcher can delete their own sessions, and a head coach can delete their team's. A deleted session's pitches are removed, and a marker stays in history showing the date, the number of pitches, and who deleted it.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and its data, email nate@knuckleballonline.com from your account's email address. We answer within 30 days. A parent can ask the same for a player they added.

Data handling commitments

For coaches and programs evaluating Knuckleball:

(a) Team-scoped access is enforced in the database

Who can read which rows is enforced by row-level security policies in the database, not only by the app's code. For example, a session is readable by its pitcher and by the coaches of the team it was charted under, and by no one else. Secret values such as invite links and approval codes can't be read directly at all; they're reachable only through purpose-built server functions.

(b) We don't sell or share your data

Knuckleball does not sell, share, license, or disclose team or player data to third parties — not scouts, not other programs, not data brokers. This is a commitment, not just a description of what we do today.

(c) No tracking, advertising, or analytics code

None is included anywhere in the app or its pages; the only outside service our pages talk to is our Supabase database.

(d) Encryption

Data is stored with Supabase, which states that “all customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS.”

(e) Who at Knuckleball can access data

Knuckleball is run by a small team. As the operators of the service, we can technically access the data it stores, as with any hosted service. We access it only for support, debugging, or safety — never to share it or for any competitive purpose.

(f) Export and deletion

On request to nate@knuckleballonline.com, answered within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its version and effective date, and tell account holders by email or in the app when the change matters to them.
